# Puff Trails brand assets v1

45 individual assets, with PNG masters and PNG/lossless WebP exports at 128, 256 and 512 maximum-edge pixels. Three app-icon previews additionally have 1024px PNG masters. Fifteen sampled palette swatches and two actual variable fonts are included.

Open [preview.html](preview.html) to browse and toggle light/dark backgrounds. See [metadata.json](metadata.json) for dimensions and file mappings, and [generation.json](generation.json) for every exact prompt and original render hash. Light/dark contact sheets are in qa/.

## Contents

- Logos: full color, wordmark, solid dark, mono black, mono white and mono purple.
- Three app-icon designs and star/cloud favicon.
- Four labeled menu buttons; separate map, globe, gift, play, star, inactive star, heart, gem, coin, energy and sparkle icons.
- Progress track/fill, blank character nameplate, six previously locked character portraits, clouds/trail and separate Puff/Trails lettering.
- Fantasy world background, hero scene and five material/background textures.
- Baloo 2 and Quicksand variable TTF files from Google Fonts, with their OFL licenses and source URLs in fonts/sources.json.

## Use and status

Non-character brand artwork was generated with the built-in image generator using the supplied board as reference. The six character portraits are exact copies of the prior locked medium masters, and three character-bearing compositions reuse those masters. The remaining brand artwork consists of generated interpretations, not pixel-identical extractions or recovered layered artwork. Alpha cleanup and proportional export preparation were applied under the user's earlier authorization. The unchanged board is in reference/; original generated renders are in sources/generated/.

These portraits reuse the previous locked family masters byte-for-byte. Character-bearing app icons and hero art are deterministic composites of those originals. File aliases from the brand board remain for compatibility: Skydoodles = Skybubs, Mossmops = Leaflings, Sunpuffs = Chirpyfluffs, Plumplets = Twirlpuffs, Peachpeeps = Peachtells. Rosybuns uses its previous heart-tuft design. The original reference board remains unchanged as historical input; its alternate character looks are superseded. Repeated stars/hearts on the board are reusable instances of the corresponding single asset.

For logos, preserve proportions and leave at least 10% of displayed width clear on all sides. Use white on dark surfaces and black/purple on light surfaces. This clear-space rule is a proposed implementation guideline, not a measurement recovered from the board.

Baloo 2 is the board's heading/button font and Quicksand its body/label font. Generated logo lettering is artwork, not editable font text. Raster button labels are baked in; use the supplied fonts for localized UI.

The Rosybun and group icons are full-bleed square composites. The simple logo icon retains rounded preview framing and white exterior margins. Icons require platform-specific preparation before store submission. Textures are not certified seamless. No editable SVG/AI logo source was supplied, so none is claimed. Character animation and expression sets are outside this brand-board pack. Final art approval and in-game/device validation remain pending.
